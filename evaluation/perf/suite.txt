# The formulas of the performance suite, one per line:  name | log | formula | why it is here
# A line "## family: description" opens a group; the report is organised by these groups.
# Logs (gen_logs.py): base has 40 A(x,y), 40 B(x,y), 10 C(x), 10 D(x,y,z) per time point,
# one time point per second, values in 0..199; rate10/rate80 change the events per time point,
# dom50/dom1000 the range, burst has sixty time points per timestamp, long has 30000 thin time
# points, tp250/tp500/tp1000 are base's shape with 250, 500 and 1000 time points.  A window bound
# b on base covers about 40(b+1) A tuples.

## join: what a conjunction costs by its shape, at one time point
join-both     | base | A(x,y) AND B(x,y)                            | equi-join on both columns: a set intersection
join-one      | base | A(x,y) AND B(y,z)                            | one shared column, the common case
join-swap     | base | A(x,y) AND B(y,x)                            | both columns shared, permuted: the index must reorder
join-first    | base | A(x,y) AND B(z,x)                            | shared column in different positions
join-chain3   | base | A(x,y) AND B(y,z) AND C(z)                   | a chain closed by a unary filter
join-chain4   | base | A(x,y) AND B(y,z) AND A(z,w) AND B(w,v)      | four-way chain: the multi-way join
join-ternary  | base | A(x,y) AND D(y,z,w)                          | binary with ternary
join-self3    | base | D(x,y,z) AND D(z,y,x)                        | ternary self-join, all columns permuted
join-star     | base | A(x,y) AND B(x,y) AND C(x) AND C(y)          | intersection with unary filters on both columns

## join_scale: the one-shared-column join as the rate and the domain grow
join-rate10   | rate10  | A(x,y) AND B(y,z)  | 10 events per predicate per time point
join-rate40   | base    | A(x,y) AND B(y,z)  | 40 (the base log)
join-rate80   | rate80  | A(x,y) AND B(y,z)  | 80: result size grows with the square of the rate
join-dom50    | dom50   | A(x,y) AND B(y,z)  | small domain: many matches per key
join-dom1000  | dom1000 | A(x,y) AND B(y,z)  | large domain: few matches, more distinct keys

## andnot: anti-joins

andnot-wide   | base | D(x,y,z) AND NOT A(x,y)                  | ternary minus binary

andnot-dom50  | dom50 | A(x,y) AND NOT B(x,y)                   | small domain: most tuples are removed
andnot-dom1000 | dom1000 | A(x,y) AND NOT B(x,y)                | large domain: almost nothing is removed
